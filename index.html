03 / About

Curious about the structure behind hard problems.

I am a PhD student in computer science at EPFL, advised by Ola Svensson and Friedrich Eisenbrand.

My research lives at the intersection of theoretical computer science and discrete mathematics. I am especially interested in approximation algorithms, linear programming relaxations, graph algorithms, and the challenge of making sophisticated methods genuinely fast.

I am part of EPFL’s Theory of Computation Laboratory and Chair of Discrete Optimization in Lausanne, Switzerland.
