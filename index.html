Lukas Vogl

I am a PhD student in computer science at EPFL, advised by Ola Svensson and Friedrich Eisenbrand.

My research is in theoretical computer science and discrete optimization. I am especially interested in approximation algorithms and graph algorithms. I've been working on correlation clustering both from a classcial approximation algorithms prespective and in a wide range of modern settings such as subliner time, streaming and dynamic.

Before joining EPFL, I obtained an MSc in Computer Science from ETH Zürich in 2022 and a BSc in Computer Science from TUM in 2019.
